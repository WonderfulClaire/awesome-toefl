# 高分语料库（Phrase Bank）

> 练习台「语料卡」数据源。按技能 + 场景分类，每条含：短语 → 用法说明。
> 原则：**用准 > 用难**。优先掌握这些，胜过硬背生僻大词。

## 写作 · 学术讨论（互动句式）
- While I see X's point that..., I think she overlooks... → 部分认同 + 推进一层（黄金句式）
- I would push back on the idea that... → 礼貌反驳
- That said, ... → 让步转折，承接对方合理点
- A compelling counterexample is... → 引出反例
- This overlooks the fact that... → 指出盲点
- rather than asking which side..., we should... → 重新框定问题
- the trade-off is worth it → 权衡结论金句
- Used well, it clearly... → 条件式收束

## 写作 · 邮件（正式语域）
- I am writing to ask whether I could... → 比 Can I 正式礼貌
- Would it be possible to...? → 委婉请求
- I would appreciate it if you could... → 高礼貌度
- I apologize for any inconvenience this may cause. → 致歉标配
- As stated in our lease / policy... → 有理有据引用规则
- I assure you this will be the only extension I request. → 主动承诺降顾虑
- I would be happy to provide a doctor's note if needed. → 主动补材料

## 写作 · 通用升级（动词/名词）
- boost morale → 替换 make people happy
- deteriorate → 替换 get worse
- capture the savings while keeping... → 平衡表述
- moves the needle → 地道"真正起作用"
- a lifeline to community → 精准比喻
- wrestle with an idea → 形象"深度思考"
- a sparring partner rather than a ghostwriter → 绝妙对比

## 口语 · 应答开头（争取思考时间）
- Well, I'd say... → 中性自然开头
- To be honest, ... → 引个人真实经历
- That's a good question. I guess... → 缓冲 + 切入
- If I think about it, ... → 边想边说
- Actually, just last week... → 自然过渡到例子

## 口语 · 展开与例子
- The reason is that... → 清晰给原因
- For instance, ... / Take ... as an example → 引具体例子
- What I mean is... → 换说法重述，防跑题
- It really matters to me because... → 加个人分量
- I learned the hard way that... → 用失败经历显真实

## 口语 · 收尾与纠错
- Anyway, that's why I... → 收束回到问题
- ...she teached — taught me a lot → 自然纠错不扣分
- So, yeah, that's basically it. → 轻松收尾
- I guess that's the gist of it. → 概括收尾

## 通用 · 衔接词（避免 Firstly/Secondly 全家桶）
- What's more → 递进
- In fact → 强调/举例
- That's why → 因果
- On the other hand → 对比
- As a result → 结果
- By contrast → 反差
- All things considered → 综合收束

## 通用 · 长短句节奏
- 一句长复杂句后接一句短句，最有「母语感」
- 例：Cities planted trees. The air got cooler, the drains breathed, and people walked more.
- 避免：全篇都是 and 连接的流水句
- 避免：每句都以 Firstly / Secondly / In conclusion 开头
