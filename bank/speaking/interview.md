# 口语访谈题库（Take an Interview）

> 新版口语第二大题，共 4 题，每题录音 **45 秒**，**无准备时间**。
> 应答结构：直接回答（1 句）→ 展开原因/细节（2–3 句）→ 具体例子或收尾（1–2 句）。
> 评分看语言不看立场，选**好说的**那边；说满 35 秒以上。

## S-I01 · 放松方式
**问题**：What do you usually do to relax after a busy day?
**类型**：经历 / 观点
**可挂素材**：一次坚持的爱好 · 压力释放
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
I usually go for a short run in the park near my home. Running helps me clear my head — after thirty minutes I basically forget whatever was stressing me out. And since I sit all day studying, my body really needs the movement. Just yesterday, after three classes back to back, a quick evening run completely reset my mood.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：去公园跑步
2. 展开：清空头脑 + 身体需要活动
3. 具体例子收尾：昨天连上三节课后跑步重置状态

## S-I02 · 喜欢的书
**问题**：What kind of book do you enjoy reading most, and why?
**类型**：偏好 / 原因
**可挂素材**：一门喜欢/讨厌的课 · 科技产品
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
I'm drawn to science fiction, especially stories that imagine how technology changes daily life. They let me think about the future without a textbook. Last month I read a novel about a city run by algorithms, and it actually changed how I look at the apps on my phone. For me, fiction is just a more fun way to study society.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：科幻
2. 展开：借想象思考未来
3. 例子：算法城市小说改变对 App 的看法

## S-I03 · 难忘的旅行
**问题**：Describe a trip that left a strong impression on you.
**类型**：经历
**可挂素材**：一次难忘的旅行 · 文化差异
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
The trip that stuck with me was a solo train journey through the mountains two years ago. I got lost near a small village and a local family invited me in for dinner, even though we barely shared a language. That night taught me more about hospitality than any class. I still think about how kind strangers can be when you least expect it.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：两年前独自山区火车旅行
2. 展开：迷路被村民收留
3. 收尾：关于善意的感悟

## S-I04 · 影响你的人
**问题**：Who has influenced you the most, and how?
**类型**：人物 / 影响
**可挂素材**：一位影响你的人（老师/家人）
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
My high-school biology teacher influenced me the most. She never just gave answers — she made us design our own experiments, even when they failed. That taught me to enjoy the process instead of chasing a grade. Because of her, I chose a science major and learned to treat mistakes as data, not failure.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：高中生物老师
2. 展开：让设计实验、包容失败
3. 结果：选理科、把错误当数据

## S-I05 · 团队合作
**问题**：Tell me about a time you solved a problem with others.
**类型**：经历
**可挂素材**：一次团队合作经历
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
Last semester our group had to build a website, but two members kept missing meetings. Instead of complaining, I set up a shared doc where everyone posted daily progress, so no one felt left out. We finished early and got the top grade. The lesson: clear, low-effort communication fixes most team problems.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：小组建网站遇成员缺席
2. 展开：建共享文档每日同步
3. 收尾：清晰低门槛沟通解决团队问题

## S-I06 · 家乡变化
**问题**：How has your hometown changed in recent years?
**类型**：地点 / 变化
**可挂素材**：你的家乡/城市
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
My hometown has changed a lot since a new subway line opened. Shops that were half-empty now have queues, and young people who moved away are coming back to open cafes. The biggest shift is energy — the city feels alive in the evenings now, whereas ten years ago everything closed by eight.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：地铁开通带来变化
2. 展开：空店变热闹、年轻人回流
3. 收尾：城市夜生活的活力对比

## S-I07 · 科技习惯
**问题**：What tech product has changed your daily routine?
**类型**：科技 / 习惯
**可挂素材**：科技产品/习惯改变
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
A smartwatch changed my mornings the most. It nudges me to stand every hour and tracks my sleep, so I actually go to bed earlier now. At first I ignored the reminders, but the weekly report shamed me into better habits. Small data, shown simply, really can shift behavior.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：智能手表
2. 展开：站立提醒 + 睡眠追踪 → 早睡
3. 收尾：简单数据改变行为

## S-I08 · 一次失败
**问题**：Describe a mistake that taught you something.
**类型**：经历 / 教训
**可挂素材**：一次失败/犯错
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
I once submitted an essay without proofreading and lost points to silly typos. Embarrassing, but useful. Now I read every draft out loud the night before — hearing the sentences catches errors my eyes skip. That one careless mistake built a habit I still use for everything I write.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：没校对论文丢分
2. 展开：现在朗读自检
3. 收尾：粗心造就沿用至今的习惯

## S-I09 · 喜欢的课
**问题**：Which class did you enjoy most, and why?
**类型**：学习 / 偏好
**可挂素材**：一门喜欢/讨厌的课 · 职业目标
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
My favorite was a statistics course, because it felt like learning a superpower. Once I understood distributions, news headlines about "studies" stopped confusing me. The professor used real scandals to show bad math, which made every lecture matter. It even pushed me toward a data career.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：统计课
2. 展开：像超能力、用丑闻教学
3. 收尾：导向数据职业

## S-I10 · 周末安排
**问题**：How do you usually spend your weekends?
**类型**：习惯 / 偏好
**可挂素材**：爱好 · 时间管理
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
I keep weekends simple: one long hike, one messy cooking experiment, and zero alarms. The hike clears my head, and cooking something new reminds me that failing a recipe is fine. Honestly, doing nothing planned is what recharges me for the week — I guard that free time tightly.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：简单三件事
2. 展开：徒步清脑 + 做饭容错
3. 收尾：无计划时间才是充电

## S-I11 · 环保行动
**问题**：What do you do in daily life to help the environment?
**类型**：观点 / 行动
**可挂素材**：科技习惯 · 家乡变化
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
The easiest thing I do is refuse single-use plastic — I carry a bottle and a tote everywhere. It sounds small, but over a year it adds up to hundreds of items. I also take the bus instead of driving when I can. None of it is heroic, but habits beat grand plans.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：拒绝一次性塑料
2. 展开：自带瓶袋 + 公交
3. 收尾：习惯胜过大计划

## S-I12 · 学习方法
**问题**：How do you learn something new effectively?
**类型**：方法 / 观点
**可挂素材**：一门喜欢的课 · 失败
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
I learn best by teaching it back. When I study a concept, I explain it out loud as if to a friend who knows nothing — the gaps in my explanation show me what I don't get. Last term I recorded these mini-lessons, and replaying them before exams worked better than re-reading notes.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：教回来
2. 展开： aloud 讲给外行听暴露漏洞
3. 例子：录音回放胜过重读笔记

## S-I13 · 推荐的地方
**问题**：Recommend a place every visitor should see in your city.
**类型**：地点 / 推荐
**可挂素材**：家乡/城市 · 旅行
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
Everyone should walk the old riverside at sunset. It's where the city slows down — street musicians, food stalls, families on bikes. I take every guest there because it shows the real rhythm of the place, not the postcard version. Go on a weekday evening and you'll blend right in.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：河边黄昏步道
2. 展开：城市慢下来的真实节奏
3. 收尾：工作日傍晚去更能融入

## S-I14 · 未来的目标
**问题**：What is one goal you hope to achieve in the next few years?
**类型**：目标 / 观点
**可挂素材**：职业目标 · 影响你的人
**限时**：45 秒

### 示范回答
My near-term goal is to publish a small research paper with my lab. I've helped on others' projects, but leading one from question to conclusion is the skill I'm missing. Even a modest result would prove I can run an investigation independently — that's the step I need before grad school.

### 应答结构
1. 直接回答：发表一篇小论文
2. 展开：缺独立主持研究的能力
3. 收尾：为读研铺路
