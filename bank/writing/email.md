# 邮件写作题库（Write an Email）

> 新版写作第二大题，限时 **7 分钟**，目标 **80+ 词**。
> 评分核心：**内容点全覆盖 + 语域正确（正式度切换）+ 格式规范**。
> 用错语域（对教授说 Hey bro）是隐形扣分重灾区。

## W-E01 · 向教授申请延期补做展示
**情境**：你本周因突发疾病错过小组展示，写邮件向教授申请在下周二补做。
**要求**：
1. 说明情况与原因
2. 提出具体补做时间
3. 询问需要走什么流程
**限时**：7 分钟
**难度**：★★☆

### 满分范文
Dear Professor Lee,

I am writing to ask whether I could make up my group presentation, which was scheduled for this Thursday. I came down with a high fever on Wednesday and was advised by the clinic to rest for a few days, so I was unable to attend class or prepare my part.

Would it be possible to present next Tuesday after the lecture instead? I have already discussed the arrangement with my group members, and they are available at that time.

Could you let me know what steps I should take to make this official? I would be happy to provide a doctor's note if needed.

Thank you very much for your understanding.

Best regards,
Li Hua

### 高分短语
- I am writing to ask whether I could... → 比 Can I... 正式且礼貌
- make up（phr.）补做 / 补考
- I came down with a fever → 比 I was sick 更具体
- Would it be possible to...? → 委婉请求
- I would be happy to provide a doctor's note if needed. → 主动补材料，显可靠

### 结构拆解
1. 开门见山：申请延期补做（点出原定时间）
2. 解释原因：突发疾病 + 医学建议（真实可信）
3. 给出方案：下周二，并已和组员协调
4. 询问流程 + 主动提供证明 + 礼貌落款

## W-E02 · 向宿舍管理员报修空调
**情境**：你宿舍空调坏了，写邮件给宿管请求维修，并说明 urgency。
**要求**：
1. 描述问题（空调不制冷、有异响）
2. 说明影响（夜间无法入睡）
3. 询问何时有空上门
**限时**：7 分钟
**难度**：★☆☆

### 满分范文
Hi Alex,

I'm writing about the air conditioner in Room 312 — it stopped cooling two days ago and now makes a loud rattling noise at night. Because of the heat, I have barely been able to sleep, which is starting to affect my classes.

Could you let me know when a repair person might be available to take a look? If a visit this afternoon is possible, that would be great.

Thanks a lot for your help!

Best,
Chen

### 高分短语
- stopped cooling → 比 is broken 更准确描述故障
- makes a rattling noise → 具体描述异响
- barely been able to sleep → 说明影响程度
- if a visit this afternoon is possible → 给时间窗口，方便对方安排
- Thanks a lot for your help! → 对 staff 用轻松但礼貌的语气

### 结构拆解
1. 开门见山：报修 + 房号
2. 描述故障：不制冷 + 异响（具体）
3. 说明影响：睡眠受扰 → 牵连上课（提升 urgency 合理性）
4. 询问时间窗 + 轻松落款（对 staff 偏 informal 合理）

## W-E03 · 向社团负责人询问招新
**情境**：你想加入摄影社团，写邮件询问招新时间和是否需要作品集。
**要求**：
1. 表达兴趣
2. 询问招新时间与方式
3. 询问是否需要提交作品集
**限时**：7 分钟
**难度**：★☆☆

### 满分范文
Hello,

I'm a second-year student and have been into photography for years. I'd love to join the Photography Club and was wondering when the recruitment period starts this semester.

Could you tell me how to sign up — is there an online form or an info session? Also, do new members need to submit a portfolio, or is everyone welcome regardless of experience?

Looking forward to hearing from you!

Best,
Wang Fang

### 高分短语
- have been into photography for years → 表达长期兴趣
- I'd love to join... → 自然的热情表达
- I was wondering when... → 委婉询问时间
- is everyone welcome regardless of experience? → 确认门槛
- Looking forward to hearing from you! → 标准收尾

### 结构拆解
1. 自我介绍 + 表达兴趣（带经历背书）
2. 询问招新时间
3. 询问报名方式 + 作品集要求（两个内容点一次问清）
4. 期待回复收尾

## W-E04 · 向教授询问论文延期提交
**情境**：你的期末论文因实验数据出问题需要更多时间，写邮件向教授申请宽限 3 天。
**要求**：
1. 说明论文进度与遇到的问题
2. 提出具体延期天数
3. 保证质量（不会再次拖延）
**限时**：7 分钟
**难度**：★★★

### 满分范文
Dear Dr. Smith,

I am writing about the final paper for your seminar, due this Friday. I have completed the literature review and most of the analysis, but I recently discovered an error in my experimental dataset that affects the results section.

Would it be acceptable to submit the paper by next Monday instead? This would give me enough time to re-run the affected trials and verify the findings. I assure you this will be the only extension I request, and the revised paper will meet all requirements.

Thank you for your patience and guidance.

Sincerely,
Maria Chen

### 高分短语
- due this Friday → 点明原截止日，显条理
- discovered an error in my dataset → 具体问题，非泛泛"busy"
- Would it be acceptable to...? → 比 Can you 正式
- re-run the affected trials → 展示你会怎么解决
- I assure you this will be the only extension → 主动承诺，降低教授顾虑

### 结构拆解
1. 开门见山：点明论文 + 原截止日
2. 进度 + 具体障碍（数据错误，非借口）
3. 提出明确延期（周一）+ 解决路径
4. 承诺不再延 + 致谢（正式语域）

## W-E05 · 向房东协商维修暖气
**情境**：公寓暖气失灵，你写邮件给房东要求维修，并提及合同义务。
**要求**：
1. 描述问题持续时长
2. 说明已尝试的临时办法
3. 引用租约要求尽快维修
**限时**：7 分钟
**难度**：★★★

### 满分范文
Dear Mr. Johnson,

The heating in my apartment (Unit 5B) has not worked for over a week, and indoor temperatures have dropped below 15°C at night. I tried using a space heater, but the circuit breaker trips whenever I run it for long.

As stated in our lease, the landlord is responsible for maintaining heating. Could you arrange for a technician to fix this as soon as possible? Given the cold weather, this has become urgent.

I appreciate your prompt attention to this matter.

Best regards,
Sofia

### 高分短语
- has not worked for over a week → 量化持续时间，显严重
- the circuit breaker trips → 具体说明临时方案失败
- As stated in our lease... → 有理有据引用合同，不卑不亢
- arrange for a technician → 明确诉求（派人修）
- I appreciate your prompt attention → 强行要求但仍礼貌

### 结构拆解
1. 问题 + 房号 + 量化时长
2. 已尝试临时方案及其失败（证明非矫情）
3. 引用租约义务 + 明确诉求（派技师）
4. 礼貌施压收尾（prompt attention）

## W-E06 · 向同学道歉并改约
**情境**：你临时有事需取消和同学的图书馆复习之约，写邮件改到明天同一时间。
**要求**：
1. 道歉并说明突发原因
2. 提议改到明天同一时间
3. 询问对方是否方便
**限时**：7 分钟
**难度**：★☆☆

### 满分范文
Hey Mia,

So sorry — something urgent just came up and I can't make our library session at 3 today. A family member needs a ride to the airport, and I just found out.

Could we meet at the same time tomorrow instead? I really don't want to fall behind on the econ problem set, so I'd prefer not to skip.

Let me know if tomorrow works for you, or suggest another slot!

Thanks,
Jess

### 高分短语
- So sorry — something urgent came up → 朋友间自然道歉
- I can't make our... session → make 表"能出席"，地道
- I really don't want to fall behind → 说明重视，显诚意
- suggest another slot → 把改期主动权交给对方
- 对同学用 Hey / Thanks（informal 合理）

### 结构拆解
1. 道歉 + 突发原因（具体：送机）
2. 提议改期（明天同时）并说明动机
3. 把决定权交还对方 + 轻松落款（对同学偏 informal）
