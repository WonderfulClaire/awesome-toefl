# 03 · 企业该优先利润还是社会责任？· 学术讨论写作范文

> 主题：商业伦理 ｜ 难度：★★★☆☆ ｜ 目标字数：~155

## 题目

**Professor**: Should a company's first duty be maximizing profit for shareholders, or should it prioritize social and environmental responsibility even at the cost of profit? Defend your position.

**Claire (student)**: Profit comes first—without money a company can't help anyone. Responsibility is a luxury for big firms.

## 范文

I'd push back on Claire's "luxury" framing—I believe responsibility and profit align more than they conflict, especially long term.

The core reason is that social and environmental responsibility *reduces risk* and builds trust. Take a tech firm that invests early in secure, private data handling: it may spend more upfront, but it avoids the reputational and regulatory disasters that sink careless competitors. That shows responsibility is not charity—it's defensive strategy.

Admittedly, small companies with thin margins must survive first, and I'm not arguing they should donate blindly. But even they benefit from treating workers and customers fairly, which lowers turnover and builds loyalty.

Unlike Claire, who sees responsibility as a cost, I'd argue it's an investment that protects and eventually grows profit.

## 中文解析

### 结构拆解
- 第 1 句：反驳 Claire 的"奢侈品"框架，立"长期统一"立场。
- 理由 + 例子：尽责降风险（数据安全的具体场景），把责任重构为"防御性战略"。
- 让步：小企业先求生，不盲捐——显周全。
- 收尾：责任是投资而非成本，呼应同学。

### 亮点表达 / 句型
- `align more than they conflict`（比冲突更一致，精准立论）
- `defensive strategy`（防御性战略，商业类加分词）
- `I'd push back on … framing`（push back on sb's framing，高级反驳套话）

### 写作思路（可借鉴点）
1. 商业伦理题的高分立场常是"辩证统一"：不说非此即彼，而说"长期看一致"。
2. 用 `Take X as an example` 举**行业具体场景**（数据安全），比泛泛"companies should be good"有力。
3. `I'd push back on …'s framing` 是学术讨论里**优雅反驳同学**的金牌句型，记牢。
