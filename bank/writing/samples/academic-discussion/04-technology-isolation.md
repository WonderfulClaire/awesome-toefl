# 04 · 科技让人更孤立还是更联结？· 学术讨论写作范文

> 主题：科技与社会 ｜ 难度：★★☆☆☆ ｜ 目标字数：~150

## 题目

**Professor**: Critics say smartphones and social media isolate us, while supporters say they connect us like never before. Where do you stand, and why?

**Kelly (student)**: I think we're more connected—I talk to friends abroad every day who I'd never meet otherwise.

## 范文

I'd side with Kelly that tech connects us, but I'd add that the tool is neutral; it depends on how we use it.

The reason I lean "more connected" is the sheer reach. A good case in point: during the pandemic, video calls let my lab group collaborate across three cities and keep our research going. That's genuine connection a generation ago couldn't have.

That said, Kelly's right to celebrate the upside, but isolation is real when screens replace face-to-face time. The fix isn't less tech—it's intentionality: using it to start conversations, not escape them.

Unlike Kelly, who frames it as purely positive, I'd stress that connection or isolation is a choice we make with the same device.

## 中文解析

### 结构拆解
- 第 1 句：接 Kelly + 提出"工具中性"的中道立场。
- 理由 + 例子：用疫情期间跨城科研协作的真实场景证明"真联结"。
- 让步：承认屏幕替代面对面会带来孤立，但解法是"有意识使用"而非弃用。
- 收尾：联结或孤立是使用者的选择，呼应同学并升华。

### 亮点表达 / 句型
- `the tool is neutral`（工具中性，辩证立论核心句）
- `sheer reach`（巨大的触达力，简洁有力）
- `intentionality`（有意而为，高级名词）

### 写作思路（可借鉴点）
1. 科技类"孤立 vs 联结"是经典题，高分立场几乎都是"中性论+看用法"，比单边站更稳。
2. 例子用**亲身可验证场景**（跨城科研）比"people can chat online"具体得多。
3. `That said, … but …` 让步桥接，自然引出"解法"，避免非黑即白。
