# 05 · 城市共享单车计划有效吗 · 综合写作范文

> 主题：城市政策 / 成本收益 ｜ 难度：★★★☆☆ ｜ 字数：~290

## 题目

**Reading（阅读）**
文章支持在城市推广**共享单车计划**，列出三项收益：① 提供廉价短途出行，缓解机动车拥堵；② 鼓励日常运动，改善公共健康；③ 以零尾气出行减少碳排放，利好环境。文章称该计划是"一举多得的城市良政"。

**Listening（听力）**
讲座逐条反驳：① 多数骑行是短途休闲而非通勤，汽车流量未减；② 许多用户本就骑车，计划并未带来"新增"运动量；③ 调度卡车与车辆制造的排放，抵消了所谓环保收益；此外还带来乱停与安全事故。三项预期目标基本都没达成。

## 范文

The reading passage praises city bike-share programs as a triple win for traffic, health, and the environment. The lecture, however, disputes each of these three claimed benefits.

First, the reading argues that bike-share eases car congestion by offering cheap short trips. The professor counters that most rides are recreational rather than commuting trips, so the number of cars on the road has barely changed.

Second, while the passage claims the program improves public health through daily exercise, the lecture points out that many users already cycled before. The program therefore creates little *new* physical activity—it merely shifts existing cyclists onto shared bikes.

Finally, the reading touts zero-emission rides as environmentally friendly. The speaker undermines this by noting that rebalancing trucks and bike manufacturing generate emissions that offset the savings. He adds that careless parking and accidents create further costs the reading ignores.

In sum, the lecture casts doubt on the reading's optimistic "triple win" assessment.

## 中文解析

### 结构拆解
- 开头用 `disputes each of these three claimed benefits` 预告"逐条反驳"三段。
- 三段精准对应阅读三项：拥堵 / 健康 / 环境，每段"阅读说X → 讲座说X没发生"。
- 收尾 `casts doubt on … "triple win"` 回扣开头。

### 亮点表达 / 句型
- `disputes each of these three claimed benefits`（claimed 暗含"声称但未证实"，精准）
- `creates little *new* physical activity`（new 是得分眼：计划没带来增量）
- `offset the savings`（抵消收益，政策成本收益类高频词）

### 写作思路（可借鉴点）
1. 政策类综合写作几乎都是"阅读列好处，讲座拆台"。每段锁定一个好处，用 `the program therefore … / merely …` 说明"预期效果未发生"。
2. 听到"多数使用者本就…"这种**替代解释**（pre-existing behavior），必写——它是推翻"新增收益"的关键。
3. 结尾可加阅读"忽略的成本"（ignores further costs），显全面。
