# 听力 Transcript L2 · 生物学讲座

> 文字稿练习素材，配合官方/免费音频使用（见[资源页](../08-resources/resources.md)）。
> 训练目标：讲座主旨 + 举例目的 + 教授态度。

**Professor:** Okay, let's talk about an animal that engineers have been quietly copying for decades — the termite. Now, when you hear "termite," you probably think of pests eating houses. But here's the puzzle that fascinated architects: a termite mound in Africa can stay within a few degrees of a comfortable temperature all day, even when it's blazing hot outside, and the termites do it without any power source.

So how? The mound isn't solid. It's basically a network of chimneys and tunnels. Hot air rises through the central chimney and pulls cooler air in from openings near the base. The shape of the tunnels controls the flow, like a lung breathing. Air goes in, gets cleaned of moisture, and circulates. No fan, no fuel — just geometry.

Now, why should we care? Because humans build skyscrapers that pump enormous energy into heating and cooling. In the 1990s, architects in Zimbabwe designed an office building inspired by this. They used a similar stack-ventilation system instead of full air conditioning for most of the year. The result: the building used about ninety percent less energy for cooling than a normal one its size.

But — and this is the honest part — copying biology is harder than it looks. That building worked because of Zimbabwe's dry climate. Try the same trick in a humid city and the "lung" just moves muggy air around. So the lesson isn't "copy the termite exactly." It's "steal the principle — use shape to move air — then adapt it to your place." Nature gives the blueprint; we still have to do the engineering.

## 题目

### Q1
**题干**：What is the main topic of the lecture?
**选项**：
A. How termites destroy wooden houses
B. How termite mounds inspire low-energy building design
C. Why African climates are good for skyscrapers
D. How to build a chimney without fuel
**答案**：B
**解析**：讲座主线：白蚁丘靠几何结构被动调温，启发人类低能耗建筑，对应 B。A 是引入的反面印象；C/D 偏窄或曲解。

### Q2
**题干**：According to the professor, how does a termite mound stay cool?
**选项**：
A. Termites fan air with their wings
B. A network of chimneys and tunnels moves air by geometry
C. The mound is made of reflective white clay
D. Cool groundwater is pumped through it
**答案**：B
**解析**：教授说 mound 是 chimneys 和 tunnels 网络，热空气上升从底部引入冷空气，"shape of the tunnels controls the flow"，即靠几何结构，对应 B。A/C/D 均未提及。

### Q3
**题干**：Why does the professor mention the Zimbabwe office building?
**选项**：
A. To prove termites can be trained
B. To show the principle applied in real architecture
C. To compare African and American climates
D. To criticize normal skyscrapers
**答案**：B
**解析**：教授举 Zimbabwe 办公楼说明"inspired by this"的 stack-ventilation 省下约 90% 制冷能耗，即把白蚁原理用于真实建筑，对应 B。A/C/D 不符。

### Q4
**题干**：What is the professor's attitude toward directly copying nature?
**选项**：
A. It always works if you copy exactly
B. It is harder than it looks and must be adapted locally
C. It should be avoided completely
D. It only works in humid cities
**答案**：B
**解析**：末段"copying biology is harder than it looks"，并强调要"adapt it to your place"，对应 B。A 错（湿度城市失效）；C 错（非完全避免）；D 错（潮湿城市反而失效）。
