# 听力 Transcript L1 · Office Hour 对话

> 文字稿练习素材，配合官方/免费音频使用（见[资源页](../08-resources/resources.md)）。
> 训练目标：听辨说话人目的 + 态度 + 细节。建议先盲听再对照本稿做题。

**Professor Diaz:** Thanks for stopping by, Kevin. You wanted to talk about the midterm?

**Kevin:** Yeah, I got the paper back, and I'm a little confused about the grade on the essay part. I thought my argument was solid, but I lost a lot of points on organization.

**Professor Diaz:** Let me pull it up. Okay — here's the thing. Your thesis was clear, and your evidence was good. The problem is the body paragraphs jumped between points without transitions. A reader can't follow why paragraph three follows paragraph two.

**Kevin:** Oh, I see. I wrote it the night before, so I just listed examples as they came to me.

**Professor Diaz:** That's the trap. The ideas were fine; the glue was missing. Two fixes: one, add a sentence at the start of each body paragraph that states its claim; two, use a bridge sentence at the end that links to the next point. Do that and the same content reads as organized.

**Kevin:** That makes sense. Is there any way to recover some of the points, or is it locked in?

**Professor Diaz:** The grade itself is final, but — here's an option — if you revise this essay using those two fixes and bring it to me by next Friday, I'll add a small bonus to your participation score. Not the essay grade, but it rewards the improvement.

**Kevin:** That's fair. I'll do it. Thanks, Professor Diaz.

## 题目

### Q1
**题干**：Why did Kevin visit the professor?
**选项**：
A. To argue that his grade was unfair
B. To ask about points lost on essay organization
C. To request an extension on the final paper
D. To discuss his thesis topic
**答案**：B
**解析**：Kevin 开头说对 essay 部分扣分"confused"，尤其 organization 丢分多，来问原因，对应 B。A 错（他说 confused 非 argue unfair）；C/D 未提。

### Q2
**题干**：According to the professor, what was actually wrong with the essay?
**选项**：
A. The evidence was weak
B. The thesis was unclear
C. Paragraphs lacked transitions and claims
D. It was submitted late
**答案**：C
**解析**：教授说 thesis 和 evidence 都好，问题是 body paragraphs 在 points 间"jumped without transitions"，且每段缺 claim 句，对应 C。A/B 是教授明确肯定的；D 未提。

### Q3
**题干**：What advice does the professor give for fixing organization?
**选项**：
A. Rewrite the whole essay with new evidence
B. Add a claim sentence and a bridge sentence per paragraph
C. Submit the essay again as is for regrading
D. Remove the third paragraph entirely
**答案**：B
**解析**：教授给两个 fix：每段开头加陈述 claim 的句子，段尾加 bridge 句连到下一段，对应 B。A/C/D 均不符。

### Q4
**题干**：What can be inferred about the essay grade itself?
**选项**：
A. The professor will raise it after revision
B. It is final, but a bonus is offered for revising
C. Kevin can retake the midterm to replace it
D. The participation score will be lowered
**答案**：B
**解析**：教授说"grade itself is final"，但若周五前按修改交来，会在 participation 加小 bonus（非改 essay 分），对应 B。A 错（essay 分不变）；C/D 无据。
