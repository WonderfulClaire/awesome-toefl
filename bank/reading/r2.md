# 阅读 Passage R2 · 蜜蜂的舞蹈语言

> 练习文本（ETS 风格，非官方真题）。训练目标：细节定位 + 推理题 + 词汇题。

In 1944, the biologist Karl von Frisch made a discovery that still surprises people: honeybees talk to one another. They do not use sounds for this, but a dance. When a forager bee finds a good patch of flowers, she returns to the hive and performs a figure-eight waggle on the vertical comb, and the details of that dance tell the waiting bees exactly where to fly.

The angle of the dance matters first. The bee aligns the straight part of her waggle run relative to gravity to point in the direction of the food — but relative to the sun, not to the hive. So if the run points straight up, the flowers lie in the direction of the sun; if it tilts thirty degrees to the left, the foraging bees know to fly thirty degrees left of the sun. The bees, it seems, carry a live map of the sky.

Distance travels in the timing. A short waggle run of half a second means food is near; a longer, ten-second performance signals a distant field. Von Frisch found the bees could report locations more than a kilometer away with useful accuracy, a feat all the more striking because the dancer has never seen the bees she is instructing.

Not every message is spatial. The vigor of the dance, the number of repeats, and even the scent the forager carries all shape the response. A rich, easy flower bed earns an enthusiastic, long performance that draws a crowd; a poor one gets a brief, half-hearted shimmy that few follow. In this way the hive allocates its workforce without a single word, balancing effort against reward the way a market might.

## 题目

### Q1
**题干**：What was von Frisch's 1944 discovery about honeybees?
**选项**：
A. They communicate through a waggle dance
B. They navigate by listening for sounds
C. They build combs in figure-eights
D. They instruct bees they have met before
**答案**：A
**解析**：首段说 von Frisch 发现蜜蜂"talk to one another"且用的是"a dance"（waggle），对应 A。B 错（不是声音）；C 错（figure-eight 是舞蹈形状非筑巢）；D 错（末段说 dancer 从未见过被指导的蜂）。

### Q2
**题干**：According to Paragraph 2, the direction of food is shown by
**选项**：
A. the speed of the waggle run
B. the angle of the run relative to the sun
C. the scent carried by the dancer
D. the number of figure-eights performed
**答案**：B
**解析**：第二段：舞蹈直线部分相对重力对齐，但"relative to the sun, not to the hive"，即相对太阳的角度指示方向，对应 B。A 是距离；C/D 非方向信息。

### Q3
**题干**：The phrase "live map of the sky" refers to the bees'
**选项**：
A. memory of the hive's layout
B. ability to read the sun's position
C. constant repainting of the comb
D. fear of flying in the dark
**答案**：B
**解析**：第二段末：蜜蜂"carry a live map of the sky"，指它们能实时根据太阳位置判断方向，对应 B"读取太阳位置的能力"。A/C/D 均不符。

### Q4
**题干**：What can be inferred about a bee performing a "brief, half-hearted shimmy" (Paragraph 4)?
**选项**：
A. The food source it found was poor
B. It was teaching bees it had met
C. The flowers were more than a kilometer away
D. It was confused about the sun's position
**答案**：A
**解析**：末段：贫乏的花源得到"brief, half-hearted shimmy"，少有蜂跟随；rich 花源才有热情长舞。故简短舞 = 花源差，选 A。B 错（未见过）；C 是远距离但用时长舞；D 无据。

### Q5
**题干**：The author compares the hive's workforce allocation to a market in order to show
**选项**：
A. bees trade flowers for honey
B. effort is balanced against reward without words
C. the hive has a leader who sets prices
D. dancing is faster than spoken language
**答案**：B
**解析**：末段末句：蜂群"balancing effort against reward the way a market might"，即无声中按收益分配劳力，对应 B。A/C 曲解 market 比喻；D 未比较速度。
